On this International Women’s Day, I find myself reflecting on the journey of women in science.

Working in STEM as a woman can sometimes feel like carrying an invisible weight. Not because we lack ability, but because we often feel the need to prove it again and again.

Many women in research know this feeling well: preparing twice as much, questioning ourselves more, working harder to ensure our voices are taken seriously.

Historically, the challenges were even more explicit. For a long time, women were excluded from scientific institutions, and some had to publish their work under male names just to be heard.

Today, progress has been made. Opportunities have expanded. Yet many pressures remain.

Women in STEM are often expected to balance multiple ideals at once: to be excellent researchers, supportive partners, devoted daughters, loving mothers, and perfect professionals. To be brilliant, but also humble. Strong, but never too assertive.

It is a quiet, complex balancing act.

And yet, despite these challenges, women continue to contribute, innovate, and push scientific discovery forward every day.

Behind many discoveries are women who are not only scientists, but also daughters, sisters, and mothers shaping the future of the next generation.

Science is stronger when every voice is heard and every mind is valued. 

So today is not only a moment to celebrate achievements, but also to recognize the perseverance, resilience, and quiet determination of women who continue to pursue knowledge and discovery.

To every woman navigating science, research, and innovation: your work matters, your voice matters, and your presence shapes the future of science.

May we continue to rise with curiosity, kindness, and strength.
