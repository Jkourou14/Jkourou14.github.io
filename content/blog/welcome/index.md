Hello, and welcome. 💗💗💗

I have been wanting to create this space for a while.

For the past few years, I have mostly shared my thoughts on LinkedIn. I love writing there, but there is always that little problem of having to fit everything into a certain number of characters. And sometimes, an idea needs more than that. It needs context, details, a little room to wander.

So I decided to make my own space.

**Thoughts Between the Lines** is where I want to write about the things that make me curious, the things I am learning, and sometimes the things I cannot stop thinking about.

I am a computational biologist and bioinformatician, currently doing my PhD, so naturally a lot of what you will find here will revolve around science.

But probably not only science.

### 🧬 What will I write about?

You will find posts about **bioinformatics, computational biology, cancer research, drug design, omics, data, and the endless rabbit holes that come with being curious about biology.**

I also want to share some of the practical things I learn along the way. Tools, resources, tutorials, little discoveries, mistakes I wish I had avoided, and things that might make someone else's journey a little easier.

And then there is **research life**.

The part that does not usually make it into a paper or a CV.

The rejected manuscripts. The experiments that refuse to cooperate. The uncertainty. The moments when you wonder whether you are actually good enough to be doing this. The small victories that nobody else sees. The strange experience of spending years trying to answer questions that sometimes create five new questions instead.

I think those things deserve to be talked about too.

### 🌸 Why "Thoughts Between the Lines"?

Because science is rarely just what is written in the final paper.

There is always something between the lines.

The questions that came before the experiment. The assumptions behind the method. The things we learn from failure. The story behind the data. The person behind the researcher.

And I suppose that is what I want this blog to be about.

Not just what I know, but what I am learning.

Not just the polished results, but the process of getting there.

And occasionally, probably, whatever scientific rabbit hole I have fallen into that week. 🐇

I do not want this to feel like another perfectly curated professional page.

I want it to feel a little more like opening a notebook and finding thoughts, questions, explanations, discoveries, and unfinished ideas inside.

So if you are a researcher, a student, someone trying to find their way into bioinformatics, or simply a curious person who enjoys learning new things, I hope you will feel at home here.

Thank you for being here.

Now, let's see where the next thought takes us. 💜

**Welcome to Thoughts Between the Lines.**

Jouhaina
